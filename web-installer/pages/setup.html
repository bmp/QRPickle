Set up QRPickle over USB

Send your callsign, location, WiFi and keys to a CYD on the USB cable, straight from this page, without joining its QRPickle-Setup hotspot, and check which board it is (v0.3.2 or newer). Works with QRPickle v0.3.1 or newer, in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Nothing you type here leaves your computer except over the USB cable to the board.

Fields you leave empty keep what the board has. Everything else (profiles, APRS, DX servers, screen cycling) is in the web console afterwards.

Board

Turn the CYD over and find the number printed on the back (where to look), then check it matches what the board detected. The board list shows which boards are tested.

Choosing a board restarts the CYD after sending (v0.3.2 or newer).

Report this board: opens GitHub's "Board report" form with the details filled in; you sign in, attach a photo of the back and submit. Nothing is sent from this page.

Accounts (optional)

This browser can't access USB serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

This page needs a secure address to reach the USB port: open it over https://, or as http://localhost:… when you serve it yourself (not 0.0.0.0 or a network address).

"No answer from the board"? Close other programs using the port (and the installer's dialog), replug the cable and try again. On Linux run stty -F /dev/ttyUSB0 115200 first (see the Install page).
