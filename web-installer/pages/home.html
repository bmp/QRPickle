The screens, as shown on the device.

QRPickle turns an ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" (CYD) into a field-friendly ham radio dashboard: DX cluster, POTA and SOTA activators, HamAlert, APRS, band conditions, satellite passes with ISS SSTV events, weather and a clock, on a touchscreen. Everything is set up from a web console on the device, and updates arrive over WiFi.
