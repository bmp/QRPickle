QRPickle space feed

QRPickle's Satellites screen lists the next passes of amateur satellites over your station, ISS SSTV events and ARISS school contacts. The orbits and the event list come from a small file that GitHub builds every 6 hours; the passes are computed on the CYD itself, so your location never leaves it.

Using your own feed

In the web console (Advanced → Satellites) you can point the device at another address serving the same JSON, for example a copy built by tools/space_feed/build_feed.py on your own machine. Leave it empty for this feed.
