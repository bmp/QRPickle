# Code of Conduct

QRPickle is a small hobby project for radio amateurs and makers. Everyone is welcome, whatever
their licence class, experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree politely and explain why.
- Assume good intent; English may not be someone's first language.
- Respect people's time: search the issues first, and give enough detail to act on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information (including addresses or locations derived from
  callsigns, APRS positions or grid squares) without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@bmp](https://github.com/bmp), through GitHub, or use
GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may edit or remove content and may block people who don't follow this code.
